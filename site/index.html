Notes on special years

    • 2022, gaps in the results. Besides being missing about 8% of finishers overall, 2022 has four stretches of the clock where results are absent (roughly 3:07–3:23, 4:12–4:15, 4:34–4:38 and 5:22–5:28). They show up as notches in that year’s hill. The data can’t say why. They barely move the typical time (4:21:46 from the results present; 4:21:44 if estimated from place numbers).
    • 2007, number of finishers. The file lists 25,521. A report citing race officials gives 24,933, which is why the page says “about 25,000”.

    About the data

    • Only runners who finished are included.
    • The hills are lightly smoothed so they are easier to read. The table is not smoothed.
    • Temperatures in the table come from Weather Spark. The source does not state units, so they are assumed to be °F.
    • Results come from chicagomarathon.com. The collection used here is Chicago Marathon Results – 1996 to 2023 by runningwithrock on Kaggle (MIT license).
