    About the data

    • Only runners who finished are included. Ages are not used on this page.
    • The chart stops at 7:00. Runners who finished later are still counted in every number; the count for the selected year is shown under the chart.
    • The hills are lightly smoothed so they are easier to read. The numbers in the table are not smoothed.
    • Temperatures come from Weather Spark. The source does not state units, so they are assumed to be °F.
    • Results come from chicagomarathon.com. The collection used here is Chicago Marathon Results – 1996 to 2023 by runningwithrock on Kaggle (MIT license).
